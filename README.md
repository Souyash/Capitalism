# CAPITANISSM — Political Intelligence, Speeches Data & Booth War-Rooms

**Capitanissm** is a multi-page political intelligence and campaign war-room web platform engineered for Indian assembly elections.

![Capitanissm Insignia](assets/logo.jpg)

---

## 🏛️ Core Pillars & Offerings

1. **Speeches & Rhetoric Data Hub (`speeches.html`)**:
   - Fact-checked assembly speech repository covering Agriculture/MSP, Youth & Recruitment, Local Infrastructure, and Welfare.
   - Built-in **Constituency Speech Framework Builder** and rhetoric counter-punchlines.

2. **Booth-Level Connection Matrix (`booth-intelligence.html`)**:
   - Polling station micro-targeting (Booths #101 to #108).
   - Demographic breakdowns, swing voter analysis, Panna Pramukh assignments, and ground grievance heatmaps.

3. **Custom Political Campaign Software (`software-suite.html`)**:
   - **BoothPulse CRM™**: Household clustering and voter grievance ticketing.
   - **VaniRhetoric Teleprompter™**: Working auto-scrolling stage prompter with speed and font controls.
   - **WarRoom Sentinel™**: D-Day polling station command, queue alerts, and EVM dispute tracking.
   - **CadreSync Broadcaster™**: Encrypted cadre communication simulation.

4. **Political Consultant for MLA Wing (`mla-consulting.html`)**:
   - Dedicated advisory division for sitting MLAs and parliamentary candidates.
   - 100-Day Campaign Victory Roadmap.
   - Confidential retainer scheduling protected by Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

5. **UPSC & WBCS Prime Scholars Cadre (`prime-members.html`)**:
   - Civil services aspirants (UPSC, WBCS, State PCS) form the intellectual core of Capitanissm.
   - Flexible research schedules and monthly honorariums (₹15,000–₹45,000/month).

6. **Induction & Eligibility Portal (`apply.html`)**:
   - Real-time client-side eligibility validator:
     - **Age 18+ Mandatory**
     - **Minimum 12th Standard Passed**
     - **Graduation Degree or Campaign Experience** for MLA Consultant role
     - Special verified badge for UPSC / WBCS / State PCS aspirants
   - Generates an official **Capitanissm Digital Provisional Member ID Pass**.

7. **Cadre War-Room Internal Portal (`dashboard.html`)**:
   - Internal workspace for verified scholars tracking tasks, speeches, and grievance resolutions.
