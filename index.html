Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact eligibility criteria to join Capitanissm?

To become a member, you must be at least 18 years of age, have completed at least 12th standard, and possess good verbal communication skills. Students preparing for UPSC, WBCS, and State PCS form our Prime Member cadre. If you want to become a Political Consultant for an MLA, you must hold a graduation degree or demonstrate prior campaign experience.

How does Capitanissm gather speech and booth data?

Our network of Prime Scholars and field cadres performs micro-audits: tracking mandi prices, unspent MLA funds, delayed infrastructure tenders, local water logging, and voter sentiment across each booth. We synthesize this raw intelligence into bullet-proof speech scripts and digital maps.

Can a sitting MLA or candidate retain Capitanissm software exclusively?

Yes. We provide single-constituency exclusivity agreements with strict non-disclosure contracts (NDAs). When an MLA retains our Custom Software (BoothPulse, WarRoom Sentinel) and Political Consultant team, no opposing candidate in that assembly constituency is onboarded.

Is the Capitanissm membership flexible for UPSC and WBCS aspirants?

Absolutely. We recognize that examination preparation requires intense study routines. Research assignments, speech writing, and booth data synthesis are scheduled flexibly so that members can earn honorariums and gain field policy experience without compromising study schedules.
